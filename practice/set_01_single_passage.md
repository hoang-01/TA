MEMORANDUM

TO: All Staff, Apex Financial Services
FROM: Facilities Management Division
DATE: October 14
SUBJECT: Upcoming Office Renovations and Temporary Relocations

As part of our continuous commitment to enhancing workplace ergonomics and expanding our digital infrastructure, the 4th-floor open workspace will undergo comprehensive remodeling beginning on Monday, November 3. The renovation project is slated to conclude by November 21.

During this three-week period, all staff members stationed on the 4th floor will be temporarily reassigned to the shared workstations on the 2nd floor. To ensure a seamless transition and minimize disruptions to daily operations, the IT department will migrate all desktop computers and internal network configurations over the upcoming weekend (October 31 – November 2). Employees are kindly instructed to clear all personal belongings from their desks by 5:00 P.M. this Friday, October 30.

Please note that meeting rooms 4A and 4B will remain inaccessible throughout the duration of the project. If you anticipate needing conference space for client briefings, please reserve the boardrooms on the 6th floor via the corporate intranet at least 48 hours in advance.

For any specific concerns regarding workstation accommodations or accessibility needs, please contact Marcus Vance at extension 4022.

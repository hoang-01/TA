# 📖 BÀI LUYỆN ĐỌC SỐ 02: VĂN BẢN ĐÔI (DOUBLE PASSAGE)

> **Hướng dẫn:** 
> 1. Đọc 2 văn bản liên kết (Trang web đặt hàng & Email khiếu nại) bên dưới.
> 2. Trả lời các câu hỏi từ **176 đến 180**.
> 3. Điền đáp án bạn chọn vào chỗ trống `👉 Đáp án của bạn: [...]`.
> 4. Nhắn vào khung chat: **"Chữa bài set 02: 176-..., 177-..., 178-..., 179-..., 180-..."**, Agent sẽ phân tích cặn kẽ câu hỏi chéo (cross-reference) và bảng Paraphrase cho bạn!

---

### VĂN BẢN 1: CHÍNH SÁCH BÁN HÀNG & ĐỔI TRẢ (WEBPAGE NOTICE)

```text
http://www.vanguardofficesupply.com/shipping-and-returns

Vanguard Office Supplies — Customer Care & Return Policy

At Vanguard Office Supplies, we strive to deliver top-tier office furniture and business technology. Please review our shipping and return guidelines below:

• Standard Shipping: Orders are processed within 24 hours and delivered via road freight in 3 to 5 business days. Free delivery is applied automatically to all domestic purchases exceeding $150.
• Expedited Freight: Available at checkout for an additional flat surcharge of $35. Guaranteed arrival within 2 business days.
• Return Guarantee: Items in original packaging may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund or merchandise exchange.
• Damaged or Defective Items: If your merchandise arrives damaged in transit, you must file an incident report online with photographic evidence within 48 hours of receipt. Vanguard will dispatch a complimentary replacement at no additional cost.
• Restocking Fee: All returns of assembled ergonomic office chairs and motorized standing desks are subject to a 10% restocking surcharge unless the return is due to manufacturing defect or shipping error.
```

### VĂN BẢN 2: EMAIL KHÁCH HÀNG (CUSTOMER INQUIRY)

```text
To: support@vanguardofficesupply.com
From: r_davis@summitconsulting.com
Date: November 12, 10:14 A.M.
Subject: Order #VG-88219 Inquiry / Damaged Delivery

Dear Customer Support Team,

On November 7, I placed an order on your website for two ErgoPro Mesh Desk Chairs ($180 each) and a box of high-yield toner cartridges ($65), selecting standard road delivery. The package was delivered to our headquarters yesterday afternoon, November 11.

Upon unboxing the first desk chair, our office manager discovered that one of the armrest adjustment brackets was badly cracked, rendering the chair unusable. The second chair and the toner cartridges arrived in flawless condition.

I have attached three high-resolution photos displaying the cracked component and the outer shipping container, which also had visible dents. Because we urgently require the replacement chair for a newly hired consultant starting next week, could you please arrange for the replacement unit to be delivered as quickly as possible? We would also appreciate clarification on how you would like us to return the damaged unit.

Sincerely,  
Rachel Davis  
Office Operations Director, Summit Consulting
```
